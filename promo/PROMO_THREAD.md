# ChatVeil Promo Thread

## Main Brief

ChatVeil is a tiny privacy layer for AI chat sidebars.

It masks old conversation titles in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Qwen, DeepSeek, and MiniMax. The current chat stays readable. Hover to peek. Pin a title unmasked from its menu.

Built for screen share anxiety.

Attach: `showcase-01-hero.png`

## Thread Replies

1/5:
The idea is simple:

Your active conversation should be visible.
Everything else can stay quiet until you need it.

ChatVeil makes chatbot sidebars private by default, and readable only on demand.

2/5:
The interaction is intentionally small:

Hover a masked title to peek.
Open the title menu and choose "Pin title unmasked" to keep one visible.
Switch mask styles from the popup.

Attach: `showcase-02-interactions.png`

3/5:
The masking is visual only.

ChatVeil does not rewrite page text, rename chats, or touch your actual history. It adds a local overlay on top of sidebar titles, so the original site keeps behaving normally.

4/5:
Privacy model:

- No network calls
- No telemetry
- No remote code
- No title uploads
- No raw title storage

Unlock keys are hashed and settings stay local by default.

Attach: `showcase-03-privacy.png`

5/5:
It ships in two forms:

- Chrome Manifest V3 extension
- Tampermonkey userscript

Supported out of the box: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Qwen, DeepSeek, and MiniMax.

Attach: `showcase-04-formats.png`
